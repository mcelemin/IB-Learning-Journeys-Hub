Container Unload📄 Policy 688625

Associates physically unload full or partial containers (carts/shuttles/pallets/bags) from trailers (single/double deck) and vans (Sprinter/7.5t), placing them into a staging/induct location and scanning the manifested container label to a pre-assigned General Area. Required in all site types (Auto, AR, Mech, Manual).

Semi-Auto Induct📄 Policy 694504

'Facer' — exclusive to automated sites. Associates manually unload shipments from a chute (previously dumped at container level via shuttle dumper), face them (label visible), and place them on a conveyor to the main conveyance for automated sortation. Only dumper volume is included in the cycle-time logic.

📊 Metrics

TierMetricUOMDefinition
T1Semi-Auto Induct Cycle Timesec/packageScan-to-scan at the auto-sorter induct (dumper volume only)
T2Container Clearance Timesec/containerFirst → last scan of a dumped container
T2Dump to Sort Timesec/packageDumper GA scan → first sorter induct scan
—DEA – Stack Late% (region)Promised induct-time compliance vs CPT

EquipmentRequirement
Safety Shoes / High-Impact Gloves / Hi-Vis1/associate
Radio1/infeed area
Ergo Mat1/infeed area
Ear plugs1/associate
Hair bobbles1/associate
Yellow Problem Solve Bags≥10/workstation
Blue Problem Solve Bags≥10/workstation

🚶 Virtual walkthrough

1Shipments arrive at chute (dumped via shuttle dumper)
2Facer unloads shipment from chute
3Face the package (label up / visible on a side)
4Place on conveyor to main conveyance
5Auto-sorter induct scans automatically → sortation

Shuttle Dump📄 Policy 692864

Container Dumper: associates move a container (cart/shuttle) from an IB staging lane/buffer to a tipper/dumper, insert it, scan to the IB QR, close doors, and activate the machine to tip/dump. Then remove the container, check for leftover parcels, and 5S the pallet/shuttle/cart. Most common in AR and Auto sites.

TierMetricUOMDefinition

T2Cycle Time — CARTsec/containerT1 filtered to CARTS
T2Cycle Time — PALLETsec/containerT1 filtered to PALLETS
T2Cycle Time — SHUTTLEsec/containerT1 filtered to SHUTTLES
—DEA – Stack Late (as induct)% (region)Container inducted on time?
—DEA – Stack Late (as divert)% (region)Package diverted on time?
—Inbound Scan Compliance (IBSC)% (target 96%)Correct IB scan of the container

Sort Slide Diverter📄 Policy 695996

SSD — critical in EU Mechanical Sort Centres. Associates manually divert parcels to different slides/lines based on classifying criteria, receiving parcels from the feeding area (ramp/chute) at a working table and directing each to its dedicated line (up to 4 lines/station). SPECIAL CASE: the single input metric IS the TPH (no cycle time, no T2/T3).

Jam Breaker📄 Policy 709902

SOP for safely clearing conveyor jams (jambusting) in Sort Centre facilities. Associates perform non-complex jambusting in designated areas (A–C) following safety protocols; RME handles complex jams (≥2.5m height, LOTO, hazardous transition points). Uses controlled stops (not E-Stop) for routine clearing. Applies in EU: DE, ES, FR, IT, UK. This is a SAFETY SOP — it does not use the T1/T2/T3 input-metric structure.

🚶 Virtual walkthrough

1Pre-jambusting safety assessment (authorization, PPE, radio, jam pole condition)
2Jam evaluation — identify source; assess if complex (≥2.5m, LOTO, hazardous transition → RME)
3Conveyor shutdown — controlled stop (never E-Stop unless injury risk); verify fully stopped
4Jam clearing — apply RSG (Ready, Steady, Go) principles, two-handed jam pole
5Post-jam — clear area, no tools left on conveyor, restart & monitor startup
